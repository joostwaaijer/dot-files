# Research and writing

Use this route for research questions, comparisons, evidence-based analysis, and document drafts. Establish the audience, question, output format, relevant period, and destination from the request. Ask for a missing choice only when it materially changes the result.

## Gather evidence in parallel

Divide the question into independent research angles with clear boundaries. Prefer available connected sources for user-specific facts and primary sources for external technical claims. Verify time-sensitive information against current owning sources. A researcher gathers evidence without editing the final deliverable or sending messages externally.

Dispatch useful independent research packages together and schedule follow-up work through [parallelism.md](parallelism.md). Accept evidence as it arrives, pass its exact source pointers forward and refill available capacity; do not repeat the same investigation across agents or wait for unrelated research once a package's own inputs are accepted. Keep a narrow indivisible question direct.

Require each research report to identify:

- The finding and its source URL or exact local/document location.
- Relevant publication/event date or version when freshness affects the claim.
- Whether the source was actually inspected, what it establishes, and its limitations.
- Contradictions, unverified claims, and inferences, labeled explicitly.

A search snippet or an unopened link is a lead, not verified evidence. Distinguish unavailable evidence from evidence that contradicts a claim. Do not fill gaps by inventing citations or measurements.

## Draft from the evidence

Wait for the prerequisite research for each dependent draft or section, rather than all research for the entire assignment. A fully evidenced section may be drafted or reviewed while unrelated research continues; unresolved cross-section dependencies remain pending. The coordinator may write the synthesis directly or delegate a writer with the required evidence brief, audience, structure, destination, and permitted edits. Give one agent ownership of the final document; use separate draft artifacts or serialized edits for contributions to the same file.

Preserve the distinction between source facts, inference, and recommendation. Cite factual claims near the text they support, and retain material uncertainty. Apply the user's language and style instructions; use the relevant artifact skill when the requested format needs it.

## Independent critique

Give a reviewer the task criteria, inspected sources or evidence reports, and draft. Ask it to check factual support, source relevance and freshness, missing requested scope, contradictions, arithmetic where relevant, and suitability for the audience. It reports specific corrections without editing the draft.

Early section or package review does not replace final integrated review of coverage, consistency and conclusions. Reuse applicable evidence from early reviews and focus the final review on integration and remaining gaps.

If research cannot establish a requested fact, state the gap or qualify the claim. If a material required check cannot be performed, report INCOMPLETE rather than implying the document is fully verified. Return justified corrections to the writer within the shared review-round limit.

Creating a requested local draft does not itself authorize sending it to recipients, posting it publicly, changing document access, or modifying connected applications. Apply the task's existing authorization at the relevant action; do not add a new approval round for actions already authorized.
